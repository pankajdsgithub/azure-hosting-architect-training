## Learning objective

To develop the ability to sit with a client, understand the business, identify missing information, convert requirements into measurable technical design inputs, evaluate Azure service alternatives, and present a secure, scalable, high-availability, cost-conscious architecture.

## Core architecture mindset

Business need first, then technology.

The architect's job is not to simply name Azure services. It is to:

- ask the right questions
- understand constraints and risk
- translate business requirements into measurable technical requirements
- select the simplest valid design
- explain trade-offs clearly
- justify decisions with operations, security, cost, and resilience considerations
